Our Background

About Agarwal Best Packers and Movers

Providing reliable, professional, and damage-free relocation services across Hyderabad and all major cities in India.

Our Journey

Built on Trust, Care, and Punctuality

Operating from Manikonda in Hyderabad, Agarwal Best Packers and Movers was established to solve the persistent challenges people face when moving homes and workplaces in India: hidden pricing, mishandled fragile goods, delayed deliveries, and unresponsive customer service.

What began in 2009 as a small two-truck operation near Manikonda has grown into one of Hyderabad's most trusted relocation brands. Our founder, a former logistics coordinator with experience in pan-India freight operations, identified a critical gap in Hyderabad's moving industry: customers were routinely overcharged, their belongings mishandled, and their calls left unanswered after booking.

Over the next decade, Agarwal Best expanded steadily — adding temperature-controlled warehousing in 2014, launching dedicated car-carrier services in 2016, and establishing our 24/7 customer helpdesk in 2018. By 2020, we had served customers in every major district of Telangana, with intercity routes spanning Hyderabad to Bengaluru, Mumbai, Pune, Chennai, Delhi, and beyond. Today our network covers 200+ cities across India, backed by a fleet of 1,500 dedicated closed-body and open-platform trucks.

Today, we operate a dedicated fleet of weather-resistant container trucks, managed by experienced packing crews who understand how to handle everything from delicate kitchen glassware and expensive LED screens to bulky hardwood furniture and corporate server cabinets.
