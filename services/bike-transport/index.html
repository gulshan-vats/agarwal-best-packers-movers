Scratch-free motorcycle and scooter shipping across India. Specializing in daily commuters, sports bikes, and heavy Royal Enfields with multi-layer wrapping and door-to-door delivery.

Scratch-Proof Packing

Triple-Layer Two-Wheeler Protection

Bikes are prone to scratches on the fuel tank, bent clutch levers, and cracked indicators when transported on open lorries. We eliminate these risks through our proven 3-layer wrapping method:

  • ✓ Layer 1 (Anti-Scratch Foam Sheet): Directly wraps painted fuel tanks and plastic fairings to shield against surface abrasion.
  • ✓ Layer 2 (Heavy Air Bubble Film): Absorbs road shocks and shields delicate speedometer consoles, headlamps, and indicators.
  • ✓ Layer 3 (Corrugated Outer Armor): Thick cardboard armor taped securely over the entire body, preventing impact contact during loading.
  • ✓ Optional Wooden Crate: Recommended for sports bikes and luxury cruisers for total 360-degree perimeter protection.

Door-to-Door Pickup & Delivery

We pick up your bike directly from your home or office in Hyderabad (Manikonda, Kondapur, Madhapur, Kukatpally, Secunderabad) and deliver it directly to your recipient address in any Indian city.

Preparation Guide

Easy 3-Step Bike Shipping Checklist

Simple preparations to ensure your bike travels smoothly and safely.

1. Maintain Minimal Fuel

Keep only about 0.5 to 1 litre of petrol in the tank so our driver can load the bike safely while meeting carrier fire-safety regulations.

2. Remove Loose Accessories

Remove external mobile holders, luggage boxes, and personal riding gear before packing so they don't loosen on transit.

3. Keep Documents Ready

Provide photocopies of the RC book, insurance certificate, and pollution certificate (PUC) along with the bike keys.
