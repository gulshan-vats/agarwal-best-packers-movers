Our Methodology

Structured 4-Phase Corporate Move

Carefully engineered workflow designed to keep every department informed and operational.

1

Site Audit & Planning

Pre-move physical survey to inventory equipment, measure service elevator dimensions, and map access loading docks.

2

Tagging & Packing

Distribution of numbered crates for files, sequential tagging of IT accessories, and anti-static wrapping of screens.

3

Secure Transit

Transport in weatherproof container vehicles secured with interior tie-down straps to absorb highway vibrations.

4

Deployment & Handover

Unloading and workstation placement according to floor-plan layout, furniture reassembly, and debris removal.
