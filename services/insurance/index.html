Transit Insurance for Relocations in Hyderabad

Protect your household assets, furniture, and vehicles against unexpected transit hazards on Indian highways. Fast policy documentation and clear claim assistance.

Coverage Tiers

Understanding Transit Insurance Options

We facilitate transparent cargo risk policies through leading national insurance underwriters:

1. Transit / Road Risk Coverage

Covers physical loss or damage directly caused by vehicular accidents, collision, fire, overturning, or total loss of the carrier vehicle during transit.

2. Comprehensive All-Risk Coverage

Covers both vehicle collision risks as well as handling damages that might occur during loading, transit, and unloading, subject to item valuation declarations and professional packing by our crew.

Important Policy Disclaimer:

Transit insurance coverage is subject to the specific terms, conditions, deductibles, depreciation schedules, and exclusions set forth by the underwriter's formal policy certificate. Agarwal Best Packers and Movers acts as a facilitator and does not underwrite insurance risk directly.

What is Covered vs. Excluded?

✓ Covered:

Highway accidents, vehicular collision, fire, container overturn, severe water/rain damage inside transit vehicle.

✕ Excluded:

Jewelry, bullion, physical currency, financial documents, customer self-packed goods, normal pre-existing wear and tear.

Step-by-Step

How to File an Insurance Claim

Should any damage occur, follow these simple steps for quick claim survey and settlement.

1

Note on Consignment

Inspect goods at delivery and write an explicit damage notation on the driver's delivery receipt (consignment copy) before signing.

2

Photo Documentation

Capture clear, high-resolution photographs and short video clips of the damaged item and its exterior packaging carton.

3

Intimate Within 48 Hrs

Email the photos, consignment note number, and item purchase bill to our helpdesk at info@agarwalbestpackersmovers.in.

4

Survey & Settlement

The insurance surveyor verifies the loss report and processes the approved claim directly to your bank account.

Insurance FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Even with expert packing and careful driving, intercity highways present uncontrollable hazards such as extreme weather, overturning accidents, or third-party vehicle collisions. Transit insurance covers these unpredictable financial losses.

Transit-Loss (Road Risk) covers losses resulting from vehicle accidents, fire, overturning, or theft during transit. All-Risk insurance also covers accidental handling damage to declared fragile items, provided packing was executed by our crew.

Jewelry, precious stones, physical cash, negotiable currency, legal property deeds, live plants, and liquids are strictly excluded. Customers must transport these personal valuables independently.

Any external damage must be noted immediately on the driver's delivery receipt (consignment copy) upon delivery and formally submitted with photographs within 24 to 48 hours.
