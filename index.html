Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about shifting rates, packing materials, and booking schedules in Hyderabad.

Charges depend on the volume of goods, packing material required, floor level and lift availability at both locations, transit distance, and any vehicle or storage needs. We provide a transparent itemized quotation with zero hidden charges.

We provide all necessary high-grade packing materials including 5-ply and 7-ply corrugated boxes, air bubble sheets, stretch film rolls, thermocol, adhesive tapes, and furniture blankets.

For local house shifting within Hyderabad, 2 to 3 days advance notice is ideal. For intercity moves or month-end shifting slots, booking 5 to 7 days in advance ensures your preferred vehicle and crew allocation.
