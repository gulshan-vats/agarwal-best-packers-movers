Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At Agarwal Best Packers and Movers ("we," "our," or "us"), we respect your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website (https://agarwalbestpackersmovers.in) or communicate with our moving team.

1. Information We Collect

When you request a moving estimate, contact us, or book a relocation, we collect the following information:

  • Contact Details: Your name, mobile phone number, and email address.
  • Relocation Information: Pickup address, destination city, tentative moving dates, property size, and item inventory descriptions.
  • Technical Data: IP address and standard browser user-agent data strictly for rate-limiting, security validation, and preventing spam submissions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data collected strictly for operational relocation purposes:

  • To prepare accurate, itemized moving estimates.
  • To call or WhatsApp you regarding survey scheduling and moving confirmations.
  • To assign transport vehicles, dispatch crew, and coordinate delivery.
  • To generate GST tax invoices, consignment receipts, and transit insurance documentation.

3. Data Confidentiality & Non-Sharing Policy

We strictly do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your phone numbers or personal information to third-party telemarketers or external lead brokers. Your information is only shared with our authorized drivers, operations supervisors, and insurance partners directly handling your specific consignment.

4. Data Retention & Security

We employ modern industry security protocols to protect your submitted enquiry data. Information transmitted through our forms is encrypted via HTTPS (SSL/TLS). Physical consignment copies and tax bills are retained only as required by Indian commercial tax and transport laws.

5. Cookies & Third-Party Services

Our website does not use tracking cookies for intrusive advertising. We may embed standard Google Maps iframes to help visitors locate our Manikonda office, which operate according to Google's standard privacy policies.
